🔥 Bezzecchi dominates in Qatar and wins the race! 🏍️🇮🇹

Marc Marquez is second: the world title is postponed, he needed a win with Martin scoring no points. Raul Fernandez is third, Ogura fourth, Bastianini fifth, Acosta sixth. Martin is seventh.
